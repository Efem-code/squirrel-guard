Checklist before the first real spray

  1. Mode should read BENCH. The pump cannot turn on in this mode.
  2. Hold a photo of a squirrel in front of the camera. Target should read Yes.
  3. Move the photo left and right, then up and down. The dot should follow in the same direction. If it moves the wrong way, flip that sign in the code.
  4. Center the photo. Aligned should turn Yes within about a second.
  5. Only after the aiming checks pass, fill the reservoir and change BENCH_MODE to false in the code, then upload again.
  6. Keep people and pets out of the spray zone during the first live test.
